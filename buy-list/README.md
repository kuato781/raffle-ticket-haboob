## Printed parts

Print from [`../stl-scad/`](../stl-scad/):

- 1 × threaded receiver
- 1 × knurled locknut
- 1 × bayonet plug
- 3 × vent baffles
- ideally 3 × layout/drill templates

Printing three layout templates is strongly recommended. It lets you place all three 82 mm baffle footprints on the lid simultaneously before cutting anything, which makes spacing repeatable and prevents adjacent baffles from colliding.

## Optional event-use supplies

- duct tape for secondary lid retention;
- spare M2.5 screws;
- spare heat-set inserts;
- spare printed plug;
- spare lid if you are building multiple raffle buckets.

## Notes

The receiver geometry was designed around the RYOBI PSBLB01B. Other blowers may require dimensional changes to the receiver bore, bayonet slots or locking-lug geometry.
