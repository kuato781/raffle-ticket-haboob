# Development / Airflow Test Videos

These videos show actual ticket behavior inside the Raffle Ticket Haboob during blower testing.

- [`haboob-bottom-view.mov`](haboob-bottom-view.mov) — bottom-oriented internal view
- [`haboob-side-view.mov`](haboob-side-view.mov) — side-oriented internal view

The test setup was intentionally simple: an iPhone was taped inside the bucket before the lid was installed and the blower was pulsed.

The footage helped identify two important behaviors that drove the final baffle design:

1. tickets could plaster directly against the exposed vent screens;
2. tickets could work into the gap between an early baffle and the lid.

Those observations led to the perimeter cage, the larger 64.5 mm screen-tab clearance zone, screw-mounted heat-set-insert bosses and additional rib preload against the underside of the lid.
